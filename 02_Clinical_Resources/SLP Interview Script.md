# Oral-D — SLP Interview Script
## 30-second framing
“I’m building a take-home tool with two modes:
1) **Video sessions** with basic completion + tracking-quality logs.
2) **Game sessions** controlled by jaw gestures with counts/duration/consistency logs.

It’s **not** intended to score technique or diagnose—at least in v1.  
I want to learn where this fits in SLP home programs and what would make it useful or unsafe.”

---

## 12 high-yield questions (SLP-specific)

### A) Clinical fit
1) **Which caseloads would actually benefit most?**  
   (peds feeding, post-stroke dysphagia, H&N cancer, trismus, neurodegenerative, voice, etc.)  
   - If you had to pick the **top 2**, what are they?

2) For those groups, what % of the home plan is **jaw-focused** vs **tongue/lip** vs **swallow maneuvers** vs **sensory/behavioral** work?

3) Where would this be **unhelpful or risky**?  
   (TMJ pain, aspiration risk, poor cognition, reflux, radiation fibrosis, etc.)

### B) Exercises and what you’d prescribe through it
4) In typical home programs, which exercises are appropriate to deliver via:
   - **Gaming mode** (jaw reps/cadence)
   - **Video mode** (instruction + completion only)
   And which ones would you **never** assign remotely?

5) For jaw-related work (if relevant): what are your standard **dose + progression rules**?
   - minutes/day, days/week, progression trigger, and **stop rules** (pain/fatigue thresholds)

### C) Outcomes and what data matters
6) What outcomes do you care about most for that population?  
   Examples: FOIS, diet advancement, MIO (mm), patient-reported function, aspiration-risk indicators, caregiver report.

7) If the device reports only:
   - duration, completion, rep counts, consistency, and “tracking quality”  
   Which of these would **change your clinical decisions**, and which are noise?

8) What’s the minimum **weekly dashboard** you’d actually use?  
   (e.g., adherence trend + red flags only)

### D) Technique, safety, and guardrails
9) What are the top **safety risks** for at-home oral-motor/jaw tasks in your practice?  
   - What guardrails should the app enforce? (daily cap, rest breaks, pain checks, contraindication checklist)

10) How do you want to handle **technique verification**?
   - Is completion-only acceptable, or do you need some verification?
   - If we can’t verify technique, what claims are still reasonable?

### E) Workflow + adoption
11) How do you prescribe and track HEP today (handouts, apps, EMR text)?  
   - What integration is realistic? PDF report? short weekly summary? something copy/paste into notes?

12) What would make you say **“yes”** to a pilot?
   - inclusion criteria, duration (2–6 weeks), primary endpoint (1–2 metrics), and what counts as success

---

## Two forcing questions (get decisive feedback fast)
- **If you had this tomorrow, what’s the first patient you’d try it on—and why?**
- **What’s the one thing that would make you refuse to use it?**  
  (liability, data quality, setup friction, safety, unclear scope)

---

## What to bring to the meeting
- 1–2 example “HEP prescriptions” (dose + schedule + what you log)
- A sample weekly report mock (even a simple bullet list)
- Your claim boundary: **adherence + activity patterns**, not swallow safety/diagnosis/strength
