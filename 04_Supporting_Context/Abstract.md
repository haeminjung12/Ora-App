## Project Abstract (Max 1000 Characters)

For children with Cerebral Palsy, oral motor therapy is vital for rehabilitation but often fails at home. Research reveals that while parents report 90% compliance, actual adherence is often below 11%, leaving clinicians without reliable data. The Ora System solves this by digitizing therapy into a dual-track platform. In Passive Mode, the device’s camera tracks facial biomarkers while the child watches engaging viral videos. In Active Mode, a wireless sensor converts therapeutic jaw movements into control inputs for existing games. This system provides clinicians with objective performance data, automates CPT 98980 reimbursement documentation, and establishes a sustainable, reimbursable standard of care.
